**Full description (max 4000):**
Scheduling across time zones shouldn't mean guessing. Time Zone Comparator shows you the same moment in every region you work with, side by side, and tells you at a glance whether people there are at work.

• Add clocks for countries and zones such as the Philippines, United States, United Kingdom, Japan, Australia, Germany, Singapore, India, UAE and Canada
• Work Hours, Off Hours and Day Off badges for each location, with your own hours and work days per clock
• Find a meeting time: see who is at work, awake or asleep, and get the best window for everyone, with one tap to set an alarm, add it to your calendar or share it
• Daylight saving warnings before clocks change
• Search any city or time zone in the world
• Snooze or dismiss alarms from the notification
• A clean home screen widget with large live clocks
• 12-hour or 24-hour time
• Drag the hour slider and pick a day of the week to plan a future meeting
• Set meeting alarms in any time zone, once, daily or Monday to Friday, with a reminder 5 to 60 minutes before
• Switch on Live Real-Time Sync to watch every clock tick
• Your clocks, alarms and settings are saved on your device
• Works offline. No ads, no accounts, no tracking

Built for freelancers, remote teams and anyone who works with clients abroad.
